How to play

Aim and release

Drag to line up your shot, then release to launch the balls.

Use the walls

Bank shots off the sides to hit more bricks. Brick numbers show how many hits are left.

Clear the objective

Check the level goal before shooting. Finish efficiently to earn more stars.

Game features

Boosters

Add balls, use a laser or blast a 3×3 area with a bomb. Unlock more powers as you play.

Lives and rescues

You can hold five lives. A final defeat costs one life, and one returns every 20 minutes. An available rewarded rescue can keep a run going.

Missions and rewards

Complete daily, weekly and monthly missions. Spend coins in the Booster Lab to refill unlocked powers.

Help & Support

Can I play offline?

You can play levels offline. Ads and the store need an internet connection.

How do I restore ad removal?

Use Restore Purchases in the store with the original store account. Coin packs are consumable.

What does ad removal cover?

It removes interstitial ads. Rewarded videos remain optional.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is stored on your device. Cloud transfer is unavailable. Settings lets you reset progress and start again from level 1.

Need help with Bricklet GO? Email us with your device model, OS and game version, and a short description of what happened.
