Privacy Policy

Last updated · November 21, 2026

This policy covers Bricklet GO on iOS and Android and this support website. It explains what is saved on your device and what services may process separately.

  • Account and app permissions
  • Local records and deletion
  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • In-app purchases
  • Other games and store links
  • Policy updates
  • Support and privacy requests

Account and app permissions

Playing does not require a Bricklet GO account. The game does not ask for access to contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone or precise location.

Local records and deletion

Level progress, stars, coins, boosters, missions and settings are kept on your device. The game also keeps records of completed transactions to prevent duplicate credit. Reset Progress in Settings clears game progress. To remove the app’s local data, use your device’s app data controls. Clearing local data does not erase transactions held by Apple or Google.

Analytics

Bricklet GO does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Unity Ads may measure ad activity as described below.

Advertising

Unity Ads provides interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Unity may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. Ad privacy choices may be available in your device settings or the ad itself.

In-app purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. When purchase verification is enabled, the game sends the product ID and store receipt or token to the verification service to validate the purchase and deliver the item.

Other games and store links

The Our Games section can open app listings in the App Store or Google Play. The destination store applies its own privacy policy.

Policy updates

This page shows the current policy. For questions about data or deletion, email support.

Support and privacy requests
